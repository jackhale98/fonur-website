About FONUR

Friends of Northeastern University Rugby is the alumni association of the Northeastern University rugby club, the Maddogs. We keep former players, families and supporters connected to the club and to each other.

What we do

  • Support the team. Alumni donations pay for travel, kit, coaching and more.
  • Bring people together. Coordinating events like alumni weekend, banquets, touch tournaments, and reunions.
  • Keep the history. The family tree records every player since 1984, and the Hall of Fame honors the club’s greatest teams and individuals.

Get involved

The alumni committee is always looking for volunteers: event hosts, class representatives, and anyone with photos or records from the early years.

Email us and get in touch!
