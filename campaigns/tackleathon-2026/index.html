You can choose more than one, and you’re welcome to pledge more than the minimum.

How it works

  1. Make your pledge with the Pledge now button above, any time until after the final whistle.
  2. Come back to this page once the game kicks off at 2:45 PM. A live leaderboard shows every player’s tackles as they happen, updated from the sideline.
  3. Right after the match we post the final tackles per player, the team’s tries and the result here.
