Alumni Weekend

The Maddogs host Babson at Carter Field at 2:45 PM, then everyone heads to Slade's Bar & Grill for the drink up.

Alumni Weekend is on Saturday, October 24, when the Maddogs host Babson at Carter Field.

Kickoff is at 2:45 PM. It’s also the day of the Alumni Weekend Tackleathon, so come cheer on every tackle.

After the game, join us for the drink up at Slade’s Bar & Grill, 958 Tremont Street, Boston.
