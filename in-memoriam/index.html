In memoriam

Remembering the Maddogs players, coaches and friends of the club we have lost.

To add someone, or to share a memory or photo, email us.

SR

Steve Rice

1970–2025

Anchor of the pack through the late 1980s and early 1990s, and mentor to the criminal era.

Steve was an anchor of the pack through the late 80s and early 90s and a great mentor to the up-and-coming criminal era. He set the tone for what it meant to be a Maddog.

Share a memory

KS

Keith "Bubba" Salome

Passed away 2024

Class of 2000. Prop of the criminal era.

Bubba was a massive, stoic prop who brought true leadership and a violent intensity to the field. He helped carry his criminal-era teammates to the 1994 Northeast Championship, the 1995 Beast of the East Championship and the 1995 and 1998 Beanpot championships. He lost a four-year battle with cancer and leaves a huge hole in the Maddog community.

Share a memory

TR

Tom Riordon

Passed away 2024

Class of 1990. Played on for years with Worcester RFC.

Tom was an avid supporter of rugby after graduation, playing with the Worcester Rugby Club for many years. He will be greatly missed by his family and the Maddog community.

Share a memory

CS

Chris Scinto

Passed away 2022

Maddog of the late 1980s. A fierce competitor and a gentle soul.

“Chris Scinto was a fierce competitor and a gentle, happy soul all at once. You could not find a nicer guy who welcomed all new Maddogs and made them feel a part of the rugby brotherhood.” — Brian Carroll

“A little guy, tough as they come and always wore a smile. Haven’t seen him in over 30 years but the bond made back then on and off the rugby pitch stands today.” — Jim Grace

“You couldn’t find a better man. Nothing dampened his spirit and he was always there for friends and teammates.” — Robert “Ogre” McCarthy

Share a memory

KH

Keith Hague

Passed away 2022

Class of 1990. Flanker in the undefeated 1990 side.

One of the original Maddogs, Keith was a 1990 Civil Engineering graduate from Cape Cod and a member of the undefeated 1990 squad. His teammates remember an intense, hard-hitting flanker who always had a smile on his face. They honored him with a gift in his name to the Professor Bruce Wallin Memorial Endowment.

Share a memory

JK

Jake Kennedy

Passed away 2020

Physical therapist, alumni-match wing and father of Maddog Zack Kennedy.

Jake healed many Maddogs as their therapist at Kennedy Brothers PT through the 90s and early 2000s, and mentored several during their co-ops. Many will remember him lining up on the wing in the annual alumni matches. His family’s Christmas in the City charity for Boston’s children is his legacy. He passed away after a battle with ALS.

JJ

John J. Hannon

Passed away 2019

Father of Maddog Jed Hannon.

John was a great friend and supporter of the club.

Share a memory

MS

Michael "Strock" Strouch

Passed away 2018

Class of 1990. The club's annual Memorial Touch Tournament began in 2019 in his honor.

Strock was one of the early Maddogs. In January 2019 alumni and undergraduates came together in the new Carter Field dome for the first Maddog Memorial Touch Tournament, held in his memory and Mike Walsh’s, with a fundraiser for their families. It has been held every year since, in their honor and for every Maddog we have lost.

MW

Mike Walsh

Class of 1990. Remembered at the first Memorial Touch Tournament in 2019.

Mike was remembered alongside Strock at the first Maddog Memorial Touch Tournament in January 2019, which raised funds for both families.

Share a memory

PB

Professor Bruce Wallin

Faculty friend of the club and namesake of the Wallin Endowment.

Professor Wallin was a mentor and friend to generations of Maddogs. The Professor Bruce Wallin Memorial Endowment, built by the alumni, supports the club every season, and he was inducted into the Hall of Fame with its first class in 2018.
