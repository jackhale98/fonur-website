Men

Bruce Wallin Memorial Endowed Fund for Men's Rugby

In 2011, the Friends of Northeastern University Rugby (FONUR) and the Northeastern rugby community lost one of its dearest friends and academic advisor, Professor Bruce Wallin. The next year, the newly established NURFC Endowment fund was renamed in his honor. Professor Wallin was a champion both on and off the pitch, who made a lifetime of giving both academic knowledge and personal and professional encouragement to anyone who was blessed to know him. This endowment will provide an annual source of funds to support the operating expenses for the Men’s Rugby team.

Men's Club Rugby Coaching and Enrichment Fund

The purpose of the fund is to supplement Club Men’s Rugby coaching salaries and enrichment activities for the club rugby team as determined by the coaches. The associate director, or their designee, will only disburse the fund for purposes that directly benefit the university in the attainment of its mission and goals. This fund will be administered by the Director of Athletics and Recreation (or any successor thereof), or their designee in collaboration with the Associate Director of Club Sports & Esports.

Where your gift goes

The men’s club runs on the support of alumni, parents and friends. Here’s what your gifts pay for.

  • Coaching. Endowing the salaries of a head coach and two assistants, so the club can recruit and keep top coaches and players get continuity from year to year.
  • Flights to nationals and invitationals. The two or three flights a year the team takes to compete at nationals and invitational tournaments.
  • Athletic trainer. A trainer at games to look after the players.
  • Strength and conditioning. A strength and conditioning coach two days a week.
  • Kit. Training and match kit, so the team looks like a team every time it takes the field.
  • Training equipment. Tackle pads, balls and the rest of the practice equipment, bought and kept in good shape.

Gifts are tax-deductible. Northeastern University’s tax ID is 04-1679980.

Women's 15s Operating Account

The Northeastern Women’s Rugby Club was founded in 1995. Since then, the team has brought home two national championship titles and have expanded to 75 players. The team is proud of their legacy and appreciates every generous donation towards their future. Your generosity will contribute to the team’s equipment, travel, and coaching salaries and ensure their continued success.

Women's 7s Operating Account

The Northeastern University Women’s Rugby 7’s Club is a Division I Club Sport Team that plays 7s during the Spring and Summer semesters and have often qualified for national tournaments in recent years. The Lady Maddogs strive to preserve rugby’s reputation as the “gentlewoman’s sport” and to be excellent in both athletics and academics. The team appreciates your generous support for their further success.
